# IELTS Writing 高频表达库 02

## Education｜教育

#IELTS #IELTS写作 #教育 #学习 #词汇搭配

本单元收录 55 条适用于 IELTS Writing Task 2 的高频表达，覆盖教育公平、学习能力、课程设计、评价方式、教师与学习环境，以及科技对教育的影响。

难度标记：

- ★ 核心：应当熟练掌握，适用范围广
- ★★ 进阶：能够提高表达的准确性和多样性
- ★★★ 高阶：适合更深入的分析，使用时要注意语境

---

## 01｜教育机会与公平

### 1. equal access to education ★

**中文：** 平等接受教育的机会

**例句：** Governments should ensure equal access to education regardless of a child's family background.

**译文：** 政府应确保儿童不论家庭背景如何，都能平等地接受教育。

**结构：** access to + 名词；`access` 在此处是不可数名词。

### 2. educational opportunities ★

**中文：** 教育机会

**例句：** Online courses can expand educational opportunities for people living in remote areas.

**译文：** 在线课程能够扩大偏远地区居民获得教育的机会。

**搭配：** provide / expand / improve educational opportunities

### 3. compulsory education ★

**中文：** 义务教育

**例句：** Free compulsory education can prevent children from being excluded because of poverty.

**译文：** 免费义务教育可以防止儿童因贫困而失学。

**搭配：** provide / complete / extend compulsory education

### 4. higher education ★

**中文：** 高等教育

**例句：** Higher education can benefit society by producing skilled professionals and supporting research.

**译文：** 高等教育可以通过培养专业人才和支持研究使社会受益。

**说明：** 通常不写 `the higher education`，除非特指某个体系。

### 5. tuition fees ★

**中文：** 学费

**例句：** Excessive tuition fees may discourage students from low-income families from attending university.

**译文：** 过高的学费可能使低收入家庭的学生不敢上大学。

**搭配：** charge / pay / raise / reduce tuition fees

### 6. financial barriers to education ★★

**中文：** 接受教育所面临的经济障碍

**例句：** Scholarships can remove financial barriers to education for disadvantaged students.

**译文：** 奖学金能够消除弱势学生接受教育所面临的经济障碍。

**结构：** a barrier to + 名词或动名词

### 7. students from disadvantaged backgrounds ★

**中文：** 来自弱势背景的学生

**例句：** Students from disadvantaged backgrounds may require additional academic and financial support.

**译文：** 来自弱势背景的学生可能需要额外的学业和经济支持。

**替换表达：** underprivileged students；前者更加中性、具体。

### 8. close the achievement gap ★★

**中文：** 缩小学业成绩差距

**例句：** Early intervention can help close the achievement gap between children from different social groups.

**译文：** 早期干预能够帮助缩小不同社会群体儿童之间的学业差距。

**搭配：** close / narrow / widen the achievement gap

### 9. promote social mobility ★★

**中文：** 促进社会流动

**例句：** Affordable education can promote social mobility by giving individuals the skills needed to improve their circumstances.

**译文：** 可负担的教育能够赋予个人改善处境所需的技能，从而促进社会流动。

**说明：** `social mobility` 指个人或家庭在社会经济阶层之间流动。

### 10. allocate public funding to education ★★

**中文：** 将公共资金分配给教育

**例句：** Governments should allocate sufficient public funding to education, particularly in underserved regions.

**译文：** 政府应向教育投入充足的公共资金，尤其是在服务不足的地区。

**结构：** allocate money / resources / funding to + 领域

### 11. address educational inequality ★★

**中文：** 应对教育不平等

**例句：** Providing every school with qualified teachers is essential to addressing educational inequality.

**译文：** 为每所学校配备合格教师，是应对教育不平等的关键。

**搭配：** address / reduce / perpetuate educational inequality

## 02｜学习能力与个人发展

### 12. acquire knowledge ★

**中文：** 获取知识

**例句：** Students should learn how to acquire knowledge independently rather than rely entirely on teachers.

**译文：** 学生应学习如何独立获取知识，而不是完全依赖教师。

**搭配：** acquire / apply / share knowledge

### 13. develop critical-thinking skills ★

**中文：** 培养批判性思维能力

**例句：** Discussing competing viewpoints helps students develop critical-thinking skills.

**译文：** 讨论相互对立的观点有助于学生培养批判性思维能力。

**说明：** 批判性思维不是单纯“批评”，而是分析证据和评估论点。

### 14. enhance problem-solving abilities ★

**中文：** 提升解决问题的能力

**例句：** Project-based learning can enhance students' problem-solving abilities.

**译文：** 项目式学习可以提升学生解决问题的能力。

**替换表达：** develop problem-solving skills

### 15. develop practical skills ★

**中文：** 培养实践技能

**例句：** Schools should help students develop practical skills that are useful beyond the classroom.

**译文：** 学校应帮助学生培养在课堂以外同样有用的实践技能。

**搭配：** develop / acquire / apply practical skills

### 16. improve academic performance ★

**中文：** 提高学业表现

**例句：** Adequate sleep and regular exercise can improve students' academic performance.

**译文：** 充足睡眠和定期锻炼能够提高学生的学业表现。

**易错提醒：** `performance` 在此处通常不可数。

### 17. achieve better learning outcomes ★★

**中文：** 取得更好的学习成果

**例句：** Smaller classes do not automatically lead to better learning outcomes.

**译文：** 较小的班级规模并不会自动带来更好的学习成果。

**说明：** `learning outcomes` 强调学习后实际获得的知识与能力。

### 18. retain information ★★

**中文：** 记住并保留信息

**例句：** Students are more likely to retain information when they actively apply what they have learnt.

**译文：** 学生主动运用所学内容时，更有可能长期记住信息。

**搭配：** absorb / process / retain information

### 19. broaden one's horizons ★★

**中文：** 开阔眼界

**例句：** Studying abroad can broaden students' horizons by exposing them to different cultures and ideas.

**译文：** 海外学习使学生接触不同文化和观念，从而开阔他们的眼界。

**易错提醒：** `horizons` 通常使用复数。

### 20. stimulate intellectual curiosity ★★★

**中文：** 激发求知欲

**例句：** An effective teacher stimulates intellectual curiosity instead of simply delivering facts.

**译文：** 优秀的教师会激发学生的求知欲，而不是简单地灌输事实。

**搭配：** stimulate / encourage / satisfy intellectual curiosity

### 21. become an independent learner ★★

**中文：** 成为能够自主学习的人

**例句：** Research assignments can encourage students to become independent learners.

**译文：** 研究型作业能够鼓励学生成为自主学习者。

**相关表达：** independent learning; learner autonomy

### 22. realise one's full potential ★★

**中文：** 充分发挥潜能

**例句：** A supportive school environment enables children to realise their full potential.

**译文：** 支持性的校园环境能够让儿童充分发挥自身潜能。

**搭配：** realise / fulfil / reach one's potential

### 23. receive a well-rounded education ★★

**中文：** 接受全面发展的教育

**例句：** Children should receive a well-rounded education that includes academic, physical and creative activities.

**译文：** 儿童应接受兼顾学术、体育和创造性活动的全面教育。

**说明：** `well-rounded` 强调多方面能力得到均衡发展。

## 03｜课程、教学与评价方式

### 24. the school curriculum ★

**中文：** 学校课程体系

**例句：** Financial literacy should be included in the school curriculum.

**译文：** 金融素养应被纳入学校课程体系。

**搭配：** design / reform / broaden the curriculum

### 25. core academic subjects ★

**中文：** 核心学术科目

**例句：** Core academic subjects remain important, but they should not crowd out arts and physical education.

**译文：** 核心学术科目仍然重要，但不应挤占艺术和体育教育的空间。

**说明：** 常指 mathematics, science, languages 等基础科目。

### 26. vocational education and training ★

**中文：** 职业教育与培训

**例句：** Vocational education and training can provide a direct route into skilled employment.

**译文：** 职业教育与培训能够为进入技能型工作提供直接途径。

**缩写：** VET；正式写作中首次出现时建议写全称。

### 27. theoretical knowledge ★

**中文：** 理论知识

**例句：** Theoretical knowledge is valuable, but students must also understand how to apply it in practice.

**译文：** 理论知识很有价值，但学生还必须懂得如何将其运用于实践。

**对比表达：** theoretical knowledge versus practical skills

### 28. put knowledge into practice ★

**中文：** 将知识付诸实践

**例句：** Work placements give students an opportunity to put their knowledge into practice.

**译文：** 工作实习为学生提供了将知识付诸实践的机会。

**替换表达：** apply theoretical knowledge in real-world settings

### 29. rely on rote learning ★★

**中文：** 依赖死记硬背

**例句：** Schools that rely heavily on rote learning may fail to develop creativity and independent thought.

**译文：** 过度依赖死记硬背的学校可能无法培养创造力和独立思考能力。

**搭配：** rely on / discourage / move away from rote learning

### 30. standardised testing ★

**中文：** 标准化考试

**例句：** Standardised testing makes comparison easier but cannot measure every aspect of a student's ability.

**译文：** 标准化考试使比较更加容易，但无法衡量学生能力的每个方面。

**英式拼写：** standardised；美式拼写为 standardized。

### 31. continuous assessment ★★

**中文：** 持续性评价；平时考核

**例句：** Continuous assessment can provide a more complete picture of a student's progress than a single examination.

**译文：** 与一次考试相比，持续性评价能够更全面地反映学生的进步。

**对比表达：** final examinations; high-stakes tests

### 32. tailor instruction to individual needs ★★★

**中文：** 根据个人需要调整教学

**例句：** Digital tools may help teachers tailor instruction to individual needs.

**译文：** 数字工具可以帮助教师根据个人需要调整教学。

**结构：** tailor A to B = 根据 B 调整 A

### 33. reduce class sizes ★

**中文：** 缩小班级规模

**例句：** Reducing class sizes may allow teachers to give pupils more individual attention.

**译文：** 缩小班级规模可以让教师给予学生更多个别关注。

**表达：** large classes; small class sizes; the student–teacher ratio

### 34. participate in extracurricular activities ★★

**中文：** 参加课外活动

**例句：** Students who participate in extracurricular activities can develop teamwork and leadership skills.

**译文：** 参加课外活动的学生能够培养团队合作和领导能力。

**结构：** participate in + 活动

## 重点表达辨析

### learn、study 与 teach

- **learn**：获得知识或技能。例：`learn a language`、`learn how to analyse evidence`
- **study**：花时间学习或研究某一学科。例：`study biology at university`
- **teach**：教授某人知识或技能。常用结构：`teach somebody something`

### student、pupil 与 graduate

- **student**：广义的学生，也常指中学生或大学生。
- **pupil**：英式英语中多指小学生或中小学生。
- **graduate**：已经完成大学学业并获得学位的人。

### skill、ability 与 knowledge

- **skill**：通过练习获得的具体技能。
- **ability**：完成某事的能力，可以是先天或后天形成的。
- **knowledge**：对事实、信息或学科内容的理解。

### access 与 opportunity

- **access to education**：能够进入或使用教育体系与资源。
- **an educational opportunity**：一次具体的学习或发展机会。

> Providing internet access does not necessarily guarantee equal educational opportunities.

提供互联网接入并不一定能保证教育机会平等。

### education 与 training

- **education**：范围较广，包含知识、思维和个人发展。
- **training**：通常以掌握某项具体工作技能为目标。

## 可直接用于作文的论证句

### 观点 1｜大学教育是否应当免费

Free university education can promote social mobility, but fully subsidising every student may place an excessive burden on public finances.

免费的大学教育能够促进社会流动，但为每位学生承担全部费用可能给公共财政造成过重负担。

### 观点 2｜考试的作用与局限

Although standardised examinations provide a convenient means of comparison, they measure only a limited range of abilities and may encourage rote learning.

虽然标准化考试提供了便于比较的方式，但只能衡量有限范围的能力，还可能助长死记硬背。

### 观点 3｜职业教育不应被低估

Vocational education should be regarded as a valuable alternative to university because it equips learners with practical skills and responds directly to labour-market needs.

职业教育应被视为大学教育之外有价值的选择，因为它使学习者掌握实践技能，并直接回应劳动力市场的需要。

### 观点 4｜科技不能取代教师

Digital technology can widen access to educational resources, but it cannot fully replace the guidance, encouragement and individual attention provided by a skilled teacher.

数字技术能够扩大教育资源的可及性，却无法完全取代优秀教师提供的指导、鼓励和个别关注。

### 观点 5｜教育不只是为了就业

Preparing students for employment is an important function of education, but schools should also develop critical thinking, creativity and civic responsibility.

帮助学生为就业做好准备是教育的重要功能，但学校还应培养批判性思维、创造力和公民责任感。
