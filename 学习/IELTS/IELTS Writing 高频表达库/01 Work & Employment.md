# IELTS Writing 高频表达库 01

## Work & Employment｜工作与就业

#IELTS #IELTS写作 #工作 #就业 #词汇搭配

本单元收录 55 条适用于 IELTS Writing Task 2 的高频表达，覆盖就业、职业发展、工作效率、职场健康、劳动公平以及未来工作形态。

难度标记：

- ★ 核心：应当熟练掌握，适用范围广
- ★★ 进阶：能够提高表达的准确性和多样性
- ★★★ 高阶：适合更深入的分析，使用时要注意语境

## 01｜就业与职业发展

### 1. employment opportunities ★

**中文：** 就业机会

**例句：** Governments should create more employment opportunities for young people, particularly in economically disadvantaged areas.

**译文：** 政府应当为年轻人创造更多就业机会，尤其是在经济欠发达地区。

**搭配：** create / provide / expand employment opportunities

### 2. job security ★

**中文：** 工作稳定性；职业保障

**例句：** For many employees, job security is more important than a high salary.

**译文：** 对许多员工而言，工作稳定性比高薪更加重要。

**搭配：** enjoy / offer / undermine job security

### 3. job satisfaction ★

**中文：** 工作满意度

**例句：** Greater autonomy can improve job satisfaction and motivate employees to perform better.

**译文：** 更大的自主权可以提高工作满意度，并激励员工表现得更好。

**搭配：** improve / increase / derive job satisfaction from

### 4. career prospects ★

**中文：** 职业前景

**例句：** Many graduates move to large cities in search of better career prospects.

**译文：** 许多毕业生前往大城市寻找更好的职业前景。

**搭配：** improve / enhance / damage career prospects

### 5. career advancement ★★

**中文：** 职业晋升；事业发展

**例句：** Access to professional training can create opportunities for career advancement.

**译文：** 获得职业培训能够创造职业晋升的机会。

**搭配：** opportunities for / barriers to career advancement

### 6. professional development ★

**中文：** 职业发展；专业能力提升

**例句：** Employers should invest in the professional development of their staff.

**译文：** 雇主应当投资于员工的职业发展。

**搭配：** invest in / support / promote professional development

### 7. gain practical work experience ★

**中文：** 获得实际工作经验

**例句：** Internships allow university students to gain practical work experience before graduation.

**译文：** 实习让大学生能够在毕业前获得实际工作经验。

**易错提醒：** `experience` 表示“经验”时通常不可数，不写 `experiences`。

### 8. acquire transferable skills ★★

**中文：** 获得可迁移技能；培养通用技能

**例句：** Part-time employment helps students acquire transferable skills such as communication and time management.

**译文：** 兼职工作帮助学生获得沟通和时间管理等通用技能。

**说明：** `transferable skills` 指可以应用于不同岗位和行业的能力。

### 9. meet job requirements ★

**中文：** 满足岗位要求

**例句：** Applicants may struggle to find employment if they do not meet basic job requirements.

**译文：** 如果求职者不符合基本岗位要求，他们可能很难找到工作。

**易错提醒：** 使用 `meet requirements`，而不是 `satisfy with requirements`。

### 10. enter the workforce ★

**中文：** 进入职场；开始参加工作

**例句：** Young adults need adequate guidance before they enter the workforce.

**译文：** 年轻人在进入职场前需要得到充分指导。

**替换表达：** enter the labour market

### 11. pursue a rewarding career ★★

**中文：** 追求一份有成就感的事业

**例句：** Education should enable individuals to pursue a rewarding career rather than merely prepare them for examinations.

**译文：** 教育应使个人能够追求有成就感的事业，而不只是让他们为考试做准备。

**说明：** `rewarding` 强调工作带来的意义、满足感或回报。

## 02｜工作效率与职场环境

### 12. a heavy workload ★

**中文：** 繁重的工作量

**例句：** A heavy workload can reduce employees' concentration and increase the risk of mistakes.

**译文：** 繁重的工作量会降低员工的专注力，并增加出错的风险。

**搭配：** have / manage / cope with a heavy workload

### 13. work to a tight deadline ★★

**中文：** 在紧迫的期限内工作

**例句：** Employees who regularly work to tight deadlines are more likely to experience stress.

**译文：** 经常需要赶工期的员工更容易感到压力。

**替换表达：** work under severe time pressure

### 14. meet a deadline ★

**中文：** 按时完成工作；赶上截止日期

**例句：** Digital tools can help teams coordinate their work and meet deadlines more efficiently.

**译文：** 数字工具可以帮助团队协调工作，更高效地按期完成任务。

**易错提醒：** “错过截止日期”是 `miss a deadline`。

### 15. maintain productivity ★

**中文：** 保持生产效率

**例句：** Regular breaks can help employees maintain productivity throughout the working day.

**译文：** 定期休息能够帮助员工在整个工作日保持效率。

**搭配：** maintain / boost / undermine productivity

### 16. improve operational efficiency ★★

**中文：** 提高运营效率

**例句：** Automation can improve operational efficiency by reducing the time spent on routine tasks.

**译文：** 自动化可以减少花在日常任务上的时间，从而提高运营效率。

**适用场景：** 科技、企业管理、人工智能与就业。

### 17. cope with a demanding workload ★★

**中文：** 应对高强度的工作量

**例句：** Without adequate support, junior staff may struggle to cope with a demanding workload.

**译文：** 如果缺乏充分支持，初级员工可能难以应对高强度的工作量。

**易错提醒：** `cope with` 后接名词或动名词。

### 18. take on additional responsibilities ★

**中文：** 承担额外责任

**例句：** Employees who take on additional responsibilities should receive appropriate recognition and compensation.

**译文：** 承担额外责任的员工应得到适当的认可和报酬。

**替换表达：** assume greater responsibility

### 19. perform well under pressure ★★

**中文：** 在压力下保持良好表现

**例句：** Although the ability to perform well under pressure is valuable, constant pressure is rarely sustainable.

**译文：** 虽然在压力下保持良好表现是一种宝贵能力，但持续承受压力通常难以长久。

### 20. boost employee morale ★★

**中文：** 提振员工士气

**例句：** Fair promotion policies can boost employee morale and reduce staff turnover.

**译文：** 公平的晋升制度可以提振员工士气并减少员工流失。

**搭配：** boost / improve / damage employee morale

### 21. foster a collaborative working environment ★★

**中文：** 营造协作型工作环境

**例句：** Open communication can foster a collaborative working environment in which employees share ideas freely.

**译文：** 开放的沟通可以营造协作型工作环境，使员工能够自由分享想法。

**替换表达：** create a supportive workplace culture

### 22. strike a healthy work-life balance ★

**中文：** 在工作与生活之间取得健康的平衡

**例句：** Flexible schedules make it easier for parents to strike a healthy work-life balance.

**译文：** 弹性工作时间让父母更容易在工作与生活之间取得健康的平衡。

**结构：** strike a balance between A and B

### 23. flexible working arrangements ★

**中文：** 灵活的工作安排

**例句：** Flexible working arrangements can improve staff retention without lowering productivity.

**译文：** 灵活的工作安排可以在不降低生产效率的情况下提高员工留任率。

**包括：** remote work, flexible hours, compressed working weeks

## 03｜压力、健康与个人满足感

### 24. workplace stress ★

**中文：** 职场压力

**例句：** Excessive workplace stress can have serious consequences for both physical and mental health.

**译文：** 过度的职场压力会对身心健康造成严重后果。

**搭配：** experience / reduce / manage workplace stress

### 25. occupational burnout ★★

**中文：** 职业倦怠

**例句：** Long hours and unrealistic expectations are major causes of occupational burnout.

**译文：** 长时间工作和不切实际的期望是导致职业倦怠的主要原因。

**替换表达：** job burnout; burnout among employees

### 26. mental well-being ★

**中文：** 心理健康；精神幸福感

**例句：** Employers have a responsibility to protect the mental well-being of their staff.

**译文：** 雇主有责任保护员工的心理健康。

**易错提醒：** `well-being` 范围比 `mental health` 更广，也包含积极的幸福状态。

### 27. job-related anxiety ★★

**中文：** 与工作有关的焦虑

**例句：** Job insecurity can lead to persistent job-related anxiety.

**译文：** 工作缺乏保障可能导致持续的工作焦虑。

**结构：** A leads to / contributes to B

### 28. work long hours ★

**中文：** 长时间工作

**例句：** People who regularly work long hours may have little time for exercise or family life.

**译文：** 经常长时间工作的人可能很少有时间锻炼或陪伴家人。

**易错提醒：** 写 `work long hours`，不要写 `work a long time` 来表达长期加班。

### 29. lead a sedentary lifestyle ★★

**中文：** 过久坐的生活方式

**例句：** Office workers often lead a sedentary lifestyle, which increases the risk of chronic disease.

**译文：** 办公室职员往往过着久坐的生活，这会增加患慢性疾病的风险。

**适用场景：** 工作、公共健康、科技与生活方式。

### 30. take a toll on one's health ★★

**中文：** 对某人的健康造成损害

**例句：** Constant overtime can take a considerable toll on employees' health.

**译文：** 持续加班会严重损害员工健康。

**结构：** take a heavy / serious / considerable toll on

### 31. come at the expense of personal well-being ★★★

**中文：** 以牺牲个人幸福与健康为代价

**例句：** Economic success should not come at the expense of personal well-being.

**译文：** 经济上的成功不应以牺牲个人幸福与健康为代价。

**结构：** A comes at the expense of B

### 32. achieve a sense of fulfilment ★★

**中文：** 获得满足感；成就感

**例句：** Meaningful work can help individuals achieve a sense of fulfilment.

**译文：** 有意义的工作可以帮助个人获得满足感。

**搭配：** a sense of personal / professional fulfilment

### 33. derive satisfaction from one's work ★★★

**中文：** 从工作中获得满足感

**例句：** Many teachers derive considerable satisfaction from helping students realise their potential.

**译文：** 许多教师从帮助学生发挥潜力的过程中获得极大的满足感。

**结构：** derive satisfaction / pleasure / benefit from

### 34. mistake busyness for productivity ★★★

**中文：** 把忙碌误认为高效

**例句：** In highly competitive workplaces, employees may mistake busyness for productivity.

**译文：** 在竞争激烈的职场中，员工可能会把忙碌误认为高效。

**结构：** mistake A for B = 把 A 误认为 B

**替换表达：** equate busyness with productivity

## 04｜收入、劳动市场与就业公平

### 35. earn a living ★

**中文：** 谋生

**例句：** Employment is not merely a way to earn a living; it can also provide social connection and a sense of purpose.

**译文：** 工作不仅是谋生的方式，也能带来社会联系和目标感。

**搭配：** earn a decent living = 获得足以维持体面生活的收入

**译文：** 不断扩大的薪酬差距会加剧收入不平等和社会紧张。

### 40. the gender pay gap ★

## 05｜科技与未来工作形态

### 46. create new jobs ★

**中文：** 创造新的就业岗位

**例句：** Although technological change eliminates some occupations, it can also create new jobs in emerging industries.

**译文：** 虽然技术变革会淘汰一些职业，但也能在新兴行业创造新的岗位。

**名词搭配：** job creation

### 47. cause job displacement ★★

**中文：** 导致岗位流失；使劳动者失去原有岗位

**例句：** Rapid automation may cause job displacement in sectors that rely heavily on routine labour.

**译文：** 快速自动化可能导致高度依赖重复劳动的行业出现岗位流失。

**说明：** `job displacement` 强调工作被技术或经济变化取代，不完全等于失业。

### 48. automate repetitive tasks ★

**中文：** 将重复性任务自动化

**例句：** Artificial intelligence can automate repetitive tasks and allow employees to focus on more creative work.

**译文：** 人工智能可以自动处理重复性任务，让员工专注于更具创造性的工作。

### 49. replace routine jobs ★

**中文：** 取代常规性工作

**例句：** Machines are more likely to replace routine jobs than occupations requiring empathy and judgement.

**译文：** 与需要同理心和判断力的职业相比，机器更可能取代常规性工作。

### 50. adapt to a changing labour market ★★

**中文：** 适应不断变化的劳动力市场

**例句：** Education systems must help young people adapt to a rapidly changing labour market.

**译文：** 教育体系必须帮助年轻人适应快速变化的劳动力市场。

**结构：** adapt to + 名词或动名词

### 51. engage in lifelong learning ★★

**中文：** 进行终身学习

**例句：** Workers may need to engage in lifelong learning to remain employable.

**译文：** 劳动者可能需要坚持终身学习，以保持就业竞争力。

**搭配：** promote / encourage / engage in lifelong learning

### 52. reskill and retrain workers ★★★

**中文：** 帮助劳动者学习新技能并接受再培训

**例句：** Governments and employers should cooperate to reskill and retrain workers whose jobs are threatened by automation.

**译文：** 政府与雇主应合作，帮助工作受到自动化威胁的劳动者学习新技能并接受再培训。

**辨析：** `reskill` 偏向学习适用于新岗位的技能；`upskill` 偏向提升现有技能。

### 53. work remotely ★

**中文：** 远程工作

**例句：** The option to work remotely can save commuting time and widen access to employment.

**译文：** 远程工作的选择可以节省通勤时间，并扩大获得工作的机会。

**名词表达：** remote work; remote working

### 54. the gig economy ★★

**中文：** 零工经济

**例句：** The gig economy offers flexibility but often provides limited job security and employment benefits.

**译文：** 零工经济带来了灵活性，但往往只能提供有限的职业保障和员工福利。

**适用场景：** 灵活就业、平台经济、劳动者权益。

### 55. precarious employment ★★★

**中文：** 不稳定、缺乏保障的就业

**例句：** Young people in precarious employment may postpone buying a home or starting a family.

**译文：** 从事不稳定工作的年轻人可能会推迟购房或组建家庭。

**替换表达：** insecure employment; unstable work

## 重点表达辨析

### job、work、career 与 occupation

- **job**：具体职位，是可数名词。例：`find a job`、`change jobs`
- **work**：工作这一活动或工作量，通常不可数。例：`find work`、`a heavy workload`
- **career**：长期职业发展历程。例：`pursue a career in medicine`
- **occupation**：职业类别，语气较正式。例：`high-risk occupations`

### productivity 与 efficiency

- **productivity**：单位时间或资源产生的成果，强调产出。
- **efficiency**：以更少的时间、成本或资源完成任务，强调投入与产出的关系。

不要把“看起来很忙”直接等同于这两个概念：

> Employees may appear busy without being genuinely productive.

### salary、wage 与 income

- **salary**：通常指按月或按年支付的固定薪资。
- **wage**：通常指按小时、天或周支付的工资。
- **income**：个人从工资、投资等来源获得的总收入。

### employable 与 employed

- **employable**：具备被雇用的能力或条件。
- **employed**：已经受雇、有工作。

> Lifelong learning helps workers remain employable.

这句话强调保持就业竞争力，而不是保证始终处于受雇状态。

## 可直接用于作文的论证句

### 观点 1｜薪资并非选择工作的唯一标准

Although salary is an important consideration, job satisfaction, career prospects and work-life balance also have a substantial influence on people's career choices.

虽然薪资是一项重要考量，但工作满意度、职业前景以及工作与生活的平衡也会显著影响人们的职业选择。

### 观点 2｜长时间工作未必提高效率

Working longer hours does not necessarily increase productivity, because fatigue can impair concentration and lead to costly mistakes.

延长工作时间并不一定能提高生产效率，因为疲劳会损害专注力并导致代价高昂的错误。

### 观点 3｜技术既会取代岗位，也会创造岗位

While automation is likely to displace workers who perform routine tasks, it can also create employment opportunities in emerging industries.

自动化虽然可能取代从事常规任务的劳动者，但也能在新兴行业创造就业机会。

### 观点 4｜政府与企业共同承担再培训责任

The responsibility for retraining displaced workers should be shared by governments and employers, both of which benefit from a skilled and adaptable workforce.

政府与雇主应共同承担培训失去原有岗位的劳动者的责任，因为双方都能从技能熟练且适应力强的劳动力队伍中受益。

### 观点 5｜灵活工作安排的价值

Flexible working arrangements can improve employees' well-being and reduce commuting, provided that clear boundaries between work and personal life are maintained.

只要能维持工作与个人生活之间的清晰界限，灵活的工作安排便可以改善员工的幸福感并减少通勤。
