TEN WORLDS OF IELTS VOCABULARY
Master 3,000+ Essential Words Through Context

PROJECT STANDARD

- Language: English only
- Format during drafting: plain-text files with Markdown-style **bold** markers
- Final format: merged TXT and EPUB
- Target: approximately 3,000–4,000 high-value IELTS words and common derivatives
- Counting rule: coverage is tracked by vocabulary item and word family, not inflated by routine plurals or verb endings
- Style: natural, coherent, academically useful, and vocabulary-dense
- Reinforcement: selected words recur across chapters, while each chapter introduces a large new set

CHAPTER COMPONENTS

1. Original long-form article
2. Target vocabulary highlighted with **double asterisks**
3. An English-only glossary
4. A short review section
5. Coverage data added to the master vocabulary ledger

DIRECTORY STANDARD

- Each chapter is stored in chapters/NN_theme/.
- article.txt contains the chapter text, glossary, and review exercises.
- vocabulary.csv contains that chapter's vocabulary records.
- validation.txt contains that chapter's automated checks.
- output/ is reserved for the final merged TXT and EPUB.
